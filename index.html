Drone Configurator

Mission‑driven drone definition and validation

About Us

Drone Configurator is a mission‑driven tool designed to translate operational requirements into physically viable drone architectures. The system is based on engineering constraints and real market‑available components.

What problem does it solve?

  • Fragmented and inconsistent drone design information
  • Builds that fail to meet mission requirements
  • High trial‑and‑error cost in payload and endurance optimization
  • Lack of physical validation in online configurators

How does it work?

  • You define mission parameters (payload, endurance, size)
  • The system estimates weight, thrust, and energy requirements
  • Real components are validated against physical limits
  • Unfeasible configurations are explicitly rejected

Status

This is an early prototype focused on validating a mission‑centric engineering approach to UAV definition. Future work will include certified builds and professional use cases.
